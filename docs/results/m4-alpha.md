# M4 results: microstructure alpha on real Nasdaq ITCH days

Protocol: ADR 0009. Generated by `make report-alpha` (`docs/results/m4-alpha.json`, full trial
list in [graveyard.md](graveyard.md)).

## Data
Seven Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH sample days (all the 2019-2020 full-day files on Nasdaq's public sample
site: 20190130, 20190327, 20190730, 20190830, 20191030, 20191230, 20200130), SHA-256 pinned in `data/checksums.json`. Fifteen liquid names:
AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, FB, INTC, CSCO, CMCSA, PEP, NVDA, NFLX, QQQ, AMD, MU, TSLA. Each day is streamed `curl | gzip -dc | tf_features` (and `tf_filter` for
M5), so the 11 GB uncompressed files never touch disk. Grid: 1 s, 09:35 to 15:55.

Walk-forward: models trained on all earlier days, tested on each of the last five days. Nothing was
tuned on a test day.

## Headline
**The order book predicts the next 10 seconds, every day, and none of it survives the spread.**

- Gradient boosting on all 17 features reaches a Spearman IC of 0.13 at 10 s, positive and stable
  on all five held-out days (0.106 to 0.138). The strongest single features are L1 order-count and
  share imbalance, then microprice deviation and L1 OFI, which is what the literature reports.
- The signal is worth 0.2 to 0.7 bps of mid move per trade, but crossing the spread and paying taker
  fees costs about 3 bps. Every one of the 38 trials loses money net, so every
  deflated Sharpe is 0 and every trial is in the graveyard.
- PBO across trials is 0.00, but that is not good news: when all trials lose, the
  "best" in-sample is just the one that trades least, and it stays least-bad out of sample.
- Annualized Sharpe numbers in the JSON are per-minute Sharpe x sqrt(390 x 252). They are hugely
  negative because the per-trade cost is nearly deterministic, so they are not shown here.

## Leakage check (training days, ridge)
| Horizon | Shuffled 5-fold IC | Purged 5-fold IC (embargo = h) |
|---|---|---|
| 10 s | 0.1098 | 0.1070 |
| 60 s | 0.0373 | 0.0111 |

At 60 s, shuffled CV more than triples the IC (0.037 vs 0.011): overlapping 60-second labels let
the model see neighbours of each test point. At 10 s the gap is small. The walk-forward numbers
above are by day, so they carry no such leak. `tests/test_alpha.py` reproduces the effect on a
constructed series (shuffled IC > 0.8, purged < 0.3).

## What this means for M5
A short-horizon signal of this size cannot pay for crossing the spread, but it might help a
strategy that *earns* the spread decide where to quote. M5 tests exactly that: an
Avellaneda-Stoikov market maker with and without an L1-imbalance skew.

## Limitations
- Seven days, one exchange, 15 names: per-day IC is shown so the dispersion is visible.
- No DeepLOB model: with seven days it would mainly measure variance (ADR 0009).
- The taker proxy ignores queue priority and impact, which would make it worse, not better.

## Reproduce
```bash
cmake --build build
for d in 01302019 03272019 07302019 08302019 10302019 12302019 01302020; do
  curl -fsS "https://emi.nasdaq.com/ITCH/Nasdaq%20ITCH/$d.NASDAQ_ITCH50.gz" | gzip -dc |
    ./build/engine/tf_features /dev/stdin data/cache/features/$d.bin \
      --symbols AAPL,MSFT,AMZN,GOOGL,FB,INTC,CSCO,CMCSA,PEP,NVDA,NFLX,QQQ,AMD,MU,TSLA
done
make report-alpha        # ~15 min on 4 cores
pytest -q tests/test_alpha.py
```
