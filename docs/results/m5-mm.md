# M5 results: Avellaneda-Stoikov market making on real Nasdaq days

Design: ADR 0010 (strategy) on the ADR 0006 L3 backtester (exact queue position, 20 µs latency,
$0.0020 maker rebate, $0.0030 taker fee). Generated by `python -m tickforge.mm_study`
(`docs/results/m5-mm.json`).

## Protocol
- 15 names, 100-share post-only quotes, max position 1,000 shares, quoting 09:35 to 15:50, flat by 15:55.
- Grid of 27 configs (gamma in {0.01, 0.1, 1}, k in {100, 200, 400} per $, alpha skew in
  {0, 1, 2} ticks), tuned on 20190130, 20190327 only. The best plain A-S config
  (gamma=1.0, k=100) and the best alpha-skewed one
  (gamma=1.0, k=100, alpha=1.0 tick) were then run once on
  the five held-out days.

## Held-out result (5 days x 15 names)
| P&L component, $ | A-S | A-S + imbalance skew |
|---|---|---|
| Spread capture | +95,126 | +100,184 |
| Adverse selection (10 s) | -144,904 | -152,442 |
| Inventory / flattening | -963 | +93 |
| Maker rebates | +11,152 | +13,743 |
| **Net** | -39,590 | -38,423 |
| Maker shares traded | 5,579,684 | 6,875,831 |
| Positive symbol-days | 11 / 75 | 20 / 75 |
| Deflated Sharpe (per symbol-day, 27 trials) | 0.000 | 0.000 |

**Both lose money.** The quotes earn about 1.7 cents/share of spread, but the flow that fills a
back-of-queue quote is informed: 10 seconds later the mid has moved about 2.6 cents/share against
it. The M4 imbalance skew helps a little (+$1.2k, 3% better, and twice as many positive
symbol-days) but nowhere near enough, and with DSR 0 it is not a demonstrated improvement.

Large-tick, deep-queue names (CSCO, CMCSA, QQQ, INTC, MU) are near break-even and positive with
the skew; high-priced names with small relative ticks (AMZN, TSLA, NFLX, FB, AAPL) lose most, which
matches the microstructure intuition that passive market making pays only where the tick is large
relative to the short-term volatility. Restricting to those names is a selection that would have to be
made on tuning days and tested again; it is left as the next experiment rather than reported here.

## What is not verified
- **Fill-model calibration against real fills** (plan's M5 verify) cannot be done without trading.
  The ADR 0006 model is exact about the queue ahead of us but assumes no impact; ADR 0010 explains
  why results are optimistic, not pessimistic. M6 adds a live-vs-replay consistency check instead.
- PPO policy (stretch goal) not attempted.
